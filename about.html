About Chill Guys XI

Our Story

Founded in 2022, Chill Guys XI started as a group of passionate gamers looking to create a competitive yet welcoming environment for virtual football enthusiasts. Over time, we've grown into a powerhouse club with 36 elite players divided into 4 fierce squads.

Our mission is to foster talent, promote sportsmanship, and deliver thrilling esports entertainment to our fans worldwide. Whether you're a seasoned veteran or a rising star, Chill Guys XI is the place to be.

The Champions Bid Tournament

This season, we are fully focused on the Champions Bid Tournament. It's a grueling test of skill, strategy, and teamwork. Our 4 internal teams—Team Ayon, Team Mahin, Team Aashiq, and Team Rayhan—are battling it out for the ultimate glory. Follow the standings to see who comes out on top!
